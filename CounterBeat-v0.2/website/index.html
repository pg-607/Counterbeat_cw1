Stop moving, then shoot on the beat

CounterBeat is a rhythm game built around counter-strafing: the trick from tactical shooters where you cancel your movement by tapping the opposite key, because your shots are only accurate while you are standing still.

A target appears in one of the windows. Run to that window, tap the opposite key to stop dead, and fire on the beat. Shoot while you are still moving and the shot misses.

Play the prototype

Controls

  • A and D — move left and right
  • Space — shoot

What works so far

  • Movement with acceleration, sliding and the counter-strafe stop
  • A target that moves between three windows on the beat
  • Scoring that checks position, speed and timing
  • Register an account and log in

Version 0.2 — early prototype. Saved scores and the rankings table are not built yet.
