How to play

  • Hold A or D to run towards the lit window.
  • Tap the opposite key to stop dead — letting go just makes you slide.
  • Press Space on the beat while you are stopped in front of the window.

A shot only counts if all three are true: you are in the lit window, your speed is near zero, and you fire within 150 ms of the beat.
